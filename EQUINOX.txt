Hold on...

Parallel.

Ah, screw it, huh?

Is that a deviation?
Falling under the pressure of sensation,
Vibrating faintly under static tension.
Just relax...
Keep moving, even when it sucks.

| #3: Postmeridian |

Tell me how
Things will start
After they end
For all of us.
And the words
That I forgot,
You'll remind me of.
And the asteroid
Crossing our gaze —
That's all we would see
There...
As bitterness
Fills up the air...


| #4: Aquamarine |

By the venom of freedom
I'm paralysed, compromised.
Under the cerulean sea
I'm swimming in the path of light
Along the bottom.
I'm watching:
As the aquamarine paradise
Blends with the skies,
The pure white clouds
Float within the glass.
Above — fresh air; breathtaking views
Are calming me down.
I'm the one who will survive
The final drought.


| #5: In sleep |

In sleep,
Life dreams itself.
Life dreams itself: the world goes on
To such diviner lunacies.
My blood sings, catching fire
In more celestial magic I adore.
The sun each morning on its way —

I long for nothing, having all.
I care not where the prizes fall,
I care not if the world forget.
Some gaps of leisure when I will
Do idle tasks; no more than that
Is what I thought I asked for.
Here my ambition dies:
The right to lie along the wall,
Not to some only, but to all —
The luxury of being proud.

Unhappy, with no delight,
I want to feel the sun, to drink the night
At home beneath the sky.
To be a beggar, and to lie —
Is not enough for me!
Not only to live, not only to be!


| #6: The vessel |

Whither and wherefore — none may know.
The wind blows, and the vessel goes
Through the pebbles, through the fossils,
Betwixt the narrow glades.
The razor cut, the froth splashes,
The lurching tilt aslant.
In the air of frozen ashes,
The Earth echoes forth the chant:
„Whither and wherefore — none may know.
The wind blows, and the vessel goes.“


| #7: Kingdom of winter |

There's nothing to do but live.
Let the soul laugh,
The heart sigh.
The long days came and went,
Yet winter, at the right hour, arrives.
I sit in the great silence as one bound,
Around, the open fields extend for miles ahead;
To the kingdom free from autumn sorrow
The frost and crystals turn their keys.
Icicles, more and more on the way
As the water drops split in the lakes' array.


| #8: The empty day |

The empty day?
How shall I arise?
The morn is grey,
All night I have dreamed of you.

With your presence, in my heart
All the place was filled.
The empty day gave way to
The night I dreamed of you.


| #9: To the dawn |

I need fear no more.
The fire burns brightly
At the open door
Of the house of memories —
Mine and yours.
I heard your voice here,
Long ago.

On the twilight trail,
Stars will come out.
And the brightest light
Will blind me one day.
And then I'll know:
It's you, and I'm left alone
No more!

Yet the night yields
To the dawn.


| #10: Whirl guy |

Gusty winds don't wanna stop.
Someone's doing wrist circles.
"Hey, whirl guy," says a serious dude,
While drinking his juice,
"Still don't have a job?" —
That was added on top.
That requires me to think —
I'm not nodding, I'm thinking.
I'm the one engaged in that.
The juice is finished.
Apart from that, I'm well prepared.
"You must have drunk a cider,"
I replied, immediately got kicked out.
I'm at home now, drinking coffee,
As the dude is screaming in his armchair
During the technical meeting,
With money only for cream!
