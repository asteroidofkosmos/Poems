REVOLUTION ALBUM

Track 1: Crystal Castle

Trapped within a mighty castle,
Burned out through the ages.
Life is written on the pages
Of shards connected in a pattern.
A lit candle on a shelf
Illuminates the way.
Pray for thunder and a storm
To wash it all away.
As fire lives inside us,
Remind yourself: It's not over.
He holds the frame in his hands
Like a weary rover,
Until he finally sings out:
"You are not to blame, my dear,
You are just a fragment here."


Track 2: One Shot

Standing in the middle of the cave,
A cigarette rests in the pocket.
A weak current floods the socket.
Rockets thrash to seize the throne.
Tension trembles in every bone.
Fooled once more by the beam,
The boat is driven by the steam.
Hands down. Open mouth.
Relief rises to the throat.
One shot. Then two in a row.
Watch it perform a messy show,
The end delivers the blow!
Voice is broken. Heart is strong.
The last step defeats us all.
Left behind — the crawling screams
Underneath your skin:
Victory is best served cold.


Track 3: Loop

Hanging in a loop,
Body broken, mind fried.
Searching for the final blow,
Can't find it anymore.
Possibilities expand,
My head's a hurricane.
The past pursues you like a soldier
who never yields.
Take one step back. Reload.
Hold your breath till clocks give up,
Exhale with all your might.
Only then will you admit:
Not all that glitters is gold,
Not everyone gets to be bold.
No one had the pros you had,
No one sees your scars.
When everything feels like an uphill struggle,
Stop!
Just think of the view from the top!


Track 4: Light of the Well

It cannot take all that long,
Drained dry by every drop.
My identity is not my fault.
I've been where the right things belong.
Now I remain there, Lord.
A common myth: nobody can see me —
Wizard of my own home,
Sort of, a one that's old.
Snowflakes are drifting down
Upon a heart. Snow brings light,
Even when it doesn't.
Everything can be sleep, of course,
Tossed to the well's source.


Track 5: Houdini

It extends to where you've been,
Where things are meant to disappear.
I'm beat.
I'm a guy with a cheat.
I'm a deep-sea submarine
With a carcass made of steel.
Slipping out of the iron drapes,
Escaping the palace of fakes
For God's sake!
Fireworks get the job done.
The Earth is rotating somewhere nearby.
Standing there to open the sky,
By saying this, I'm doing it
On the sly.


Track 6: Stars

From Venus,
The wind has brought them here.
Brightness is a calming thing,
There is a place for this.
Parachuting like dandelion seeds
Onto a silky sheet,
Pinned to a memory field.
Step by step, they’re growing up,
Some slow, some fast enough.
Twinkle, whilst the coin is flipping.
Someday they will fade out.
A supernova pays no fee,
You can bank on it for free.


Track 7: Waterfall

Surging up from the ground,
Falling each time,
Like a whetted sword,
Past the shoulders of giant rocks.
It continues downward
Beneath the lowest-lying stone.
It narrows
Inside the soul's shadow.
It requires a certain scope
To squeeze through
And cleave the wall.


Track 8: Faceless

Today is not the same as yesterday.
The world split in half.
Somewhere inside my mind,
The truth and the lie
Are sitting back to back.
All the cards are face up.
Forget the word "before".
Who's the real king of the floor?
Life is an arrow, drawn
From destiny's bow.


Track 9: Fog of War

Dust in a tailored hat,
Drowned in a smoking blues.
In the middle of an ocean of death,
Where people have been.
Now it's all nowhereness.
Buzzing midges and tenacious spirits —
Participants of an amaranthine temple,
Dislodged by the harsh limits.
The sand is full of tragic traces...
Don't give up! A new bloody flood is coming.
You're the only one who will stop it.
Gather the others and help them survive
After the whistle.


Track 10: Letter to the Future

Foreword to the future "me",
Yet my spirit ain't sell.
Nothing in this world is on a prairie,
Add to that an underground cell.

But you're sitting by the window,
As morning rays grasp the kingdom.
Fascinating views outside of it
Can be seen.

When gray clouds appear again,
Just remember: they will wane.
Find one more striking light on the way,
See an anger of ferocious pain.

Once on top, there is no way back.
Odd habits can seldom occur.
It doesn't matter, when you're strong
To outrun obscureness:
By unlacing the ties of your dream,
By being the main hero of your own film.
